How this project uses personal data

This student project collects only the personal information needed to create an account, identify the logged-in user, connect the account to a small business profile, and support password reset.

Data collected: username, full name, email, business name, business type, password hash, and privacy consent timestamp.

Purpose: account access, inventory ownership, profile display, and password reset.

Security: passwords are stored as hashes. Password reset tokens are stored as hashes and expire after 30 minutes.

Your rights: UK GDPR includes rights such as being informed, access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and complaint to the ICO.

This notice supports transparency and data minimisation, but a live business deployment still needs a complete privacy policy, retention policy, data controller details, and operational procedures.
